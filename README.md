## User Journeys

1. **Journey 1: Finding Store Information**
   - The visitor enters the homepage (`index.html`).
   - Reads the store description and checks operating hours.
   - Navigates to `contact.html` to view the store's physical address and map.

2. **Journey 2: Browsing Products and Placing an Order**
   - The visitor navigates to `products.html` via the main navigation menu.
   - Compares items in the price list and featured product cards.
   - Clicks the "Order Now" button on a desired item to open the order modal form.
   - Fills out the form details (name, phone, address) and submits the order.

3. **Journey 3: Submitting a Feedback Request**
   - The visitor navigates to `contact.html`.
   - Fills out the contact form with their inquiry or message.
   - Clicks the submit button to send the feedback.
